TinyTab AI check

When a step waits too long during playback, TinyTab asks an AI model what the page shows. Then it logs out, reloads, or starts the run again. It also checks each run's start page for an account still signed in.

What the AI gets
  • The tab's address without the query, its title, and the first 1,500 characters of its visible text.
  • The names of the buttons, links and fields on screen. Never what is typed in a field.
  • The step TinyTab waits to do, as words ("click the Next button").
  • A screenshot, only with "Send a screenshot too" on.
